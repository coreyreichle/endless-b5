# EARTH ALLIANCE PRESIDENTIAL ELECTION, 2258
#
# Chronology note:
# The Santiago / Crane election is established in February 2258, but no reliable
# exact election day is known. February 28 is used here as the implementation
# date because "Midnight on the Firing Line" is placed on that date in the
# working chronology and contains the election material. This is a chronology
# implementation choice, not a claim that February 28 is explicitly canonical.
#
# January 1, 2258:
# - Earth Alliance ship chatter begins discussing the Santiago / Crane election.
# - Earth Alliance spaceports begin carrying election discussion.
#
# February 28, 2258:
# - Campaign chatter stops.
# - A one-time election-result bulletin announces Luis Santiago's re-election
#   with Morgan Clark as Vice President.
# - Earth Alliance ship and spaceport chatter switches to post-election reactions.
#
# March 1, 2258:
# - Election-specific ship chatter ends and the normal EA hail phrase is restored.
#
# The dated government events only replace Earth Alliance's friendly hail phrase.
# Other governments and hostile / disabled hails are untouched.
# ===========================================================================

# FEBRUARY 2258 ELECTION RESULT ANNOUNCEMENT
# ===========================================================================
mission "b5 story calendar ea election result 2258"
	landing
	invisible
	to offer
		has "b5 story start"
		"year" == 2258
		"month" >= 2
		not "b5 ea election result announced"
	on offer
		conversation
			`As you finish the routine post-flight checks, the public information channel cuts across the local terminal feed with the Earth Alliance seal and the tone reserved for major government announcements.`
			`"Earth Alliance Election Commission results are now final. Luis Santiago has won re-election as President of the Earth Alliance. Morgan Clark will serve as Vice President."`
			`The announcement continues with turnout figures and concession statements. Around you, the reaction ranges from cheers to quiet disappointment to the weary relief of people glad that months of campaigning are finally over.`
			action
				set "b5 ea election result announced"
			branch decline
