# Babylon 5 Part 1 - story calendar event: Ambassador Kosh arrives.
#
# January 1-14, 2257:
# If the player enters / launches into Epsilon, Kosh's Vorlon transport arrives
# through hyperspace.
#
# January 15, 2257:
# A dated fallback event records Kosh's canonical arrival even if the player did
# not witness the ship entering Epsilon.
#
# The next suitable landing on Babylon 5 acknowledges the ambassador's arrival.
mission "b5 story calendar kosh arrival witnessed"
	entering
	invisible
	source
		system "B5 Epsilon"
	destination "B5 Babylon 5"
	to offer
		has "b5 story start"
		"year" == 2257
		"month" == 1
		"day" <= 14
		not "b5 kosh arrival witnessed"
		not "b5 kosh arrived"
	on accept
		set "b5 kosh arrival witnessed"
		log "A Vorlon diplomatic vessel entered the Epsilon system through a jump point and proceeded toward Babylon 5."
	npc
		government "Vorlon Empire"
		personality
			entering
			uninterested
			pacifist
			fleeing
		system "B5 Epsilon"
		ship "Vorlon Transport" "Vorlon Diplomatic Vessel"
	on complete
		set "b5 kosh arrived"
event "b5 kosh canonical arrival 2257"
	date 15 1 2257
	set "b5 kosh arrived"

mission "b5 story calendar kosh arrival acknowledgement"
	landing
	invisible
	source "B5 Babylon 5"
	to offer
		has "b5 story start"
		has "b5 kosh arrived"
		not "b5 kosh arrival acknowledged"
	on offer
		conversation
			branch "witnessed" "not witnessed"
				has "b5 kosh arrival witnessed"
			label "witnessed"
			`Babylon 5 traffic control is still unusually busy when you dock. The strange vessel you saw emerge into Epsilon has been granted a secure berth under diplomatic clearance.`
			`Station personnel speak quietly about its passenger: Ambassador Kosh, the Vorlon representative assigned to Babylon 5. Even among officers accustomed to alien delegations, the arrival has attracted more curiosity than anyone is willing to admit.`
			action
				log "I witnessed a Vorlon diplomatic vessel enter the Epsilon system. On Babylon 5, I learned that its passenger was Ambassador Kosh, the Vorlon representative assigned to the station."
				set "b5 kosh arrival acknowledged"
			branch decline
			label "not witnessed"
			`Babylon 5 traffic control is unusually busy when you dock. A Vorlon vessel has arrived under diplomatic clearance and has been granted a secure berth with minimal station access.`
			`Its passenger is Ambassador Kosh, the Vorlon representative assigned to Babylon 5. Details are scarce, and station personnel seem to have learned quickly that asking questions about the Vorlons produces very few answers.`
			action
				log "Ambassador Kosh, the Vorlon representative, arrived aboard Babylon 5 under diplomatic clearance."
				set "b5 kosh arrival acknowledged"
			branch decline
