# Babylon 5 main storyline - Mission 3.
# Offered at the Babylon 5 spaceport after Mission 2.
#
# The three Narn transports are mission NPCs. `save accompany` means all three
# must survive and be present with the player in the Narn system for completion.

mission "b5 mission 3 narn escort"
	name "Mission 3: Narn Convoy Escort"
	description "Escort three Narn transports from Babylon 5 to the Narn homeworld."
	source "B5 Babylon 5"
	destination "B5 Narn Homeworld"
	to offer
		has "b5 story start"
		has "b5 mission 2 complete"
		not "b5 mission 3 complete"
	on offer
		dialog "Earth Alliance liaison has a new assignment for you. Three Narn transports are preparing to depart Babylon 5 for the Narn homeworld. You are to escort the convoy through hyperspace and see all three ships safely to Narn."
	on accept
		set "b5 mission 3 convoy underway"
		log "Babylon 5" "Earth Alliance Orders" "Mission 3: Escort three Narn transports from Babylon 5 to the Narn homeworld. All three transports must survive and arrive with you."
	npc save accompany
		government "Narn Regime"
		personality
			escort
		ship "Narn G'Quon Shuttle" "Narn Transport One"
		ship "Narn G'Quon Shuttle" "Narn Transport Two"
		ship "Narn G'Quon Shuttle" "Narn Transport Three"
	on complete
		conversation
			`All three Narn transports have arrived safely at the Narn homeworld. The convoy crews acknowledge your escort. As the final manifests are being transferred, a Narn official approaches and asks you to wait.`
			`A few moments later another Narn enters the room. His clothing is considerably more elaborate, and the reaction of everyone around him makes his importance immediately obvious.`
			`"Lt. Commander <last>. A moment, if you please."`
			`The newcomer studies you for a second before extending his hand. "I am G'Kar, ambassador of the Narn Regime to Babylon 5. I understand I have you to thank for ensuring that three Narn transports arrived here in substantially the same condition in which they departed."`
			`He gives you a broad smile. "Considering the recent history between our governments, I find that... encouraging."`
			choice
				`"I was simply carrying out my orders."`
					goto orders
				`"Your people were under attack. There wasn't much to decide."`
					goto stayed
			label orders
			`G'Kar laughs. "Yes. EarthForce officers are very fond of saying that." His expression becomes more serious. "Nevertheless, you remained with the convoy when remaining became dangerous. The Narn do not easily forget such things."`
			goto warning
			label stayed
			`G'Kar's smile fades into something more thoughtful. "An admirably uncomplicated answer. Unfortunately, the universe has a habit of becoming complicated whether we wish it to or not."`
			`He inclines his head. "You remained with the convoy when remaining became dangerous. The Narn do not easily forget such things."`
			label warning
			`G'Kar turns to leave, then pauses. "And Lt. Commander? Raiders rarely attack without believing there is something worth taking. I would give some thought to what they expected to find."`
			`With that, G'Kar departs. Your orders remain unchanged: return to Babylon 5 and report the Raider interception to Commander Ivanova.`
			choice
				`Return to your ship.`
					accept
		clear "b5 mission 3 convoy underway"
		set "b5 mission 3 complete"
		set "b5 met G'Kar"
		log "Babylon 5" "Earth Alliance Orders" "The Narn convoy arrived safely. On Narn, Ambassador G'Kar personally thanked you for protecting the transports and cautioned that the Raiders may have expected to find something worth taking. Return to Babylon 5 and report the interception to Commander Ivanova."
	on fail
		clear "b5 mission 3 convoy underway"
		dialog "One of the Narn transports has been lost. The escort assignment has failed."
# Mission 3 first-jump Raider ambush.
#
# This is deliberately a separate invisible entering mission. It offers after the
# player regains control following the first jump out of Epsilon. Because no NPC
# system is specified, the Raider NPC group starts in that current system.
mission "b5 mission 3 first jump raider ambush"
	name "Mission 3 Raider Ambush"
	invisible
	non-blocking
	entering
	destination "B5 Narn Homeworld"
	source
		not system "B5 Epsilon"
	to offer
		has "b5 mission 3 convoy underway"
		not "b5 mission 3 raider ambush triggered"
	on offer
		set "b5 mission 3 raider ambush triggered"
		log "Babylon 5" "Earth Alliance Orders" "Raiders have intercepted the Narn convoy immediately after its first hyperspace jump."
	to complete
		never
	npc
		government "Raiders"
		personality
			hunting
			staying
		fleet "B5 Raider Fighter Pair"
