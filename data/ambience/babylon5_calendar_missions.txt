# Babylon 5 Part 1 - Non-story calendar ambience missions.
#
# PURPOSE:
# Recurring, calendar-driven work that is independent of main-story progression.
# Story-dependent calendar missions belong with the story mission files instead.
#
# MUTAI TRANSPORT:
# Each August, Earth Alliance authorizes passenger transport to Babylon 5 for
# people traveling in connection with the annual Mutai tournament.
#
# - 2258: Narn, Centauri, and League / Independent pickup orders.
# - 2259 onward: the same three orders plus an Earth Alliance-origin order.
# - Orders may repeat throughout August.
# - The player remains an Earth Alliance officer; passengers do not employ them.
# - All variants below match the current 1,000-credit weekly salary tier.
# ===========================================================================

mission "b5 calendar mutai narn transport 1000"
	name "EA Calendar Order: Mutai Transport"
	job
	repeat
	passengers 3 6
	description "Earth Alliance liaison channels have authorized transport for <passengers> traveling from <origin> to Babylon 5 in connection with the annual Mutai tournament. Carry them to Babylon 5. Completion carries one standard week of service compensation: <payment>."
	source
		government "Narn Regime"
	destination "Babylon 5"
	to offer
		has "b5 story start"
		"b5 ea weekly salary" == 1000
		"year" >= 2258
		"month" == 8
	on accept
		dialog "Earth Alliance authorization is confirmed. The Narn travelers report aboard with their documents and little interest in ceremony. Their destination is Babylon 5 for the annual Mutai tournament."
	on complete
		payment 1000
		dialog "The Narn travelers disembark into the growing tournament crowds on Babylon 5. The authorized transport order is closed, and <payment> in standard service compensation is credited to your account."
