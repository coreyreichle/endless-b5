# Babylon 5 Part 1 - ambient in-flight radio chatter.
#
# These phrases provide non-gameplay ambient transmissions from friendly ships.
# Endless Sky uses a government's "friendly hail" phrase for random in-flight
# chatter as well as friendly ship hails.
#
# DESIGN RULES:
# - Flavor only. No mission hints, deadlines, rewards, or actionable intelligence.
# - No story progression or Shadow War spoilers.
# - Faction voice should be recognizable without requiring lore knowledge.
# - Vorlon and Shadow governments are intentionally omitted. Casual chatter would
#   undermine their intended tone.
# - Raider chatter is intentionally omitted for now; hostile hail work can be
#   developed separately.
#
# Because these are attached to ship governments, regional flavor emerges from
# the fleets present in a system. A Narn ship speaks with a Narn voice even when
# encountered outside Narn space, which is appropriate for intercepted traffic.


# ===========================================================================
# EARTH ALLIANCE
# ===========================================================================

phrase "b5 friendly earth alliance"
	word
		"Earthforce traffic control reminds all civilian vessels to maintain assigned approach vectors."
		"Routine navigation advisory: verify your jump coordinates before entering the beacon corridor."
		"Earth Alliance Customs reminds captains that undeclared cargo may be subject to inspection."
		"Merchant traffic is advised to keep identification transponders active while approaching Alliance facilities."
		"Earthforce recruitment offices are accepting applications throughout Alliance space. Service guarantees responsibility."
		"Colonial Affairs reports another increase in passenger traffic to the outer colonies."
		"Attention all ships: priority docking lanes are reserved for military and emergency traffic."
		"Alliance navigation control reports normal jump-point activity. Maintain safe separation."
		"Freighter control, this is commercial transport seven-one-four. Requesting revised docking clearance."
		"Anyone else getting static on the civilian band, or is our comm array finally giving up?"
		"Next time somebody says this run is routine, remind me how many hours we spent waiting for clearance."
		"We're carrying machine parts, agricultural equipment, and three crates that somehow have six different customs forms."
		"Tell maintenance the port maneuvering thruster is drifting again. I am not docking this thing sideways."
		"Passenger liner Meridian to local control: requesting departure clearance on the outbound commercial lane."
		"Earthforce patrol to civilian traffic: maintain course. This is a routine identification sweep."
		"There's a rumor the station commissary finally received real coffee. I'll believe it when I taste it."
		"Cargo tug six, clear the approach lane. Some of us have schedules to pretend we're keeping."
		"Alliance beacon network is operating normally. Report navigation anomalies to the nearest traffic authority."
		"Long-range haulers are reporting heavier traffic than usual along the inner colony routes."
		"Whoever designed this cargo manifest never had to load a ship."

# CENTAURI REPUBLIC
# ===========================================================================

phrase "b5 friendly centauri"
	word
		"Centauri traffic control welcomes registered vessels. Unregistered vessels may expect a considerably less gracious reception."
		"Imperial commerce office reminds captains that proper customs documentation prevents unfortunate delays."
		"House courier requesting priority clearance. The seals are diplomatic and the schedule is not negotiable."
		"My dear captain, if your cargo were truly priceless, you would have hired a better ship."
		"The spoo is properly aged, properly sealed, and apparently more important than every passenger aboard."
		"Control, inform the dockmaster that House representatives do not wait behind livestock transports."
		"A merchant without patience is merely a customer with a ship."
		"We were promised a reception committee. I see two fuel tenders and a customs inspector."
		"Centauri liner Aurelia requesting approach clearance and a berth appropriate to diplomatic passengers."
		"Tell the purser that if one more case of wine is missing, I will personally inspect the crew quarters."
		"I assure you, the customs declaration is complete. Whether it is understandable is an entirely different matter."
		"The Republic has endured for centuries. Surely this traffic queue cannot last longer."
		"Dock control, our passengers have been waiting twenty minutes and have already begun writing complaints."
		"That freighter's paint scheme is either very fashionable or the result of an industrial accident."
		"Commercial traffic advisory: noble delegations have priority on the inner approach lanes."
		"Yes, the cargo is insured. No, that does not mean you may handle it carelessly."
		"The captain says this route is prestigious. The crew says it is long."
		"Centauri courier to control: sealed correspondence aboard, requesting immediate departure authorization."
		"I have heard three different explanations for the delay. I admire the creativity."
		"Please remind the loading crew that antique furnishings are not ballast."
