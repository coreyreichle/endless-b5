# Endless B5 - Earth Alliance colony expansion
# Adds landable worlds to existing Earth Alliance systems without changing
# hyperspace links, patrol fleets, or the existing faction homeworld definitions.
#
# Design rules:
# - Shipyards remain limited to the primary faction homeworld (Earth).
# - Industrial / scientific colonies may have an Earth Alliance outfitter.
# - Agrarian / frontier colonies rely mainly on commodity trade.
# - Planet attributes are intended for future ambient mission filtering.

# -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

# -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
