# Always-on Vorlon Homeworld defense.
#
# This mission is intentionally independent of both the Story and Testbed start.

# Vorlon defense fleet in the Vorlon Homeworld system.
#
# Endless Sky mission NPCs spawn after mission activation when the player next
# departs a planet. With `staying`, the defense force never leaves its home system.

	name "Vorlon Homeworld Permanent Defense"
	landing
	invisible
	non-blocking
	to complete
		never
	npc
		government "Vorlon Homeworld Defense"
		personality
			heroic
			hunting
			staying
			uninterested
		system "Vorlon Homeworld"
		fleet "B5 Vorlon Homeworld Defense"
