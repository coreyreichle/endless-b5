# Endless B5

**Babylon 5 for Endless Sky**

Endless B5 is a total-conversion-style plugin for **Endless Sky** that recreates the Babylon 5 universe as a playable setting.

The project is intended to provide a self-contained Babylon 5 experience using the Endless Sky engine, with its own galaxy, factions, ships, equipment, economies, news, and story campaign.

Development is currently focused on the primary story, which establishes the setting and gameplay foundation for a larger campaign intended to progress through the **Shadow War** and, eventually, the **Earth Civil War**.

> **Target Endless Sky version:** 0.11.2
> **Status:** Active development / playable development build

---

## Current Development Status

Endless B5 is currently playable as a sandbox, but it is **not yet a finished campaign or content-complete release**.

The foundation of the Babylon 5 universe is now in place and continues to be expanded. Current development includes the major factions, their territories, ships, equipment, colonies, economies, and the opening portion of the story campaign.

This project is also being developed as a **self-contained setting**. It should live alongside the base game, with neither interferring in the other.

Expect maps, economies, balance, artwork, and story content to continue changing throughout development.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

The current development build allows you to:

* Start a career in the Babylon 5 universe.
* Explore territory belonging to the major powers.
* Visit Babylon 5 and a growing number of colonies and inhabited worlds.
* Fly Babylon 5 ships and use setting-specific equipment.
* Trade between systems using a developing regional economy.
* Encounter faction-specific governments, fleets, news, and world-building.
* Play the currently implemented opening story missions.
* Explore the setting as a sandbox while the larger campaign is being developed.

---

## Factions

The core powers currently represented include:

* **Earth Alliance**
* **Narn Regime**
* **Centauri Republic**
* **Minbari Federation**

These factions have their own territories and supporting gameplay content, but they should not yet be considered complete.

Additional systems, colonies, ships, fleets, equipment, economic differences, and faction-specific content will continue to be added and refined.

---

## Galaxy and Economy

The Babylon 5 galaxy is under active expansion.

Major faction territories have been established, and we are in the process of making the universe feel more "lived-in" rather than just waypoints.

Current work includes:

* New colony worlds and inhabited systems.
* Planet and spaceport descriptions.
* Faction-specific governments and system ownership.
* Commodity pricing and regional trade.
* News and flavor content.
* Improved organization of map data by faction.
* Continued refinement of hyperspace routes and travel.

The economy is functional but remains under development and will require continued balancing as more systems and commodities are introduced.

## Current Development Priorities

Current work is primarily focused on:

* Replacing temporary and AI-generated artwork with original or appropriately licensed assets.
* Continuing development of the opening campaign.
* Expanding colonies, inhabited worlds, and faction territories.
* Developing and balancing regional economies and trade.
* Expanding faction-specific ships, equipment, fleets, and encounters.
* Preventing unintended vanilla Endless Sky content from appearing in the Babylon 5 setting.
* Improving/ensuring compatibility with current Endless Sky releases.
* Fixing bugs, inconsistencies, and data errors as the universe expands.

## Artwork

Some artwork currently included in the project is temporary, including AI-generated material used during development.

These assets are **placeholders** and are intended to be replaced.

Creating or sourcing suitable original artwork is one of the project's major outstanding tasks. Contributions from artists interested in Babylon 5 and Endless Sky are welcome.

## Installation

Endless B5 is an **Endless Sky plugin** and requires Endless Sky to play.

The plugin currently targets:

**Endless Sky 0.11.2**

Download or clone this repository into your Endless Sky plugins directory and restart Endless Sky.

The plugin should then appear in the game's plugin list.

Because this project is under active development, using the version of Endless Sky listed above is recommended. Compatibility with older or newer versions is not guaranteed.

## Project Goals

The goal of Endless B5 is not simply to add Babylon 5 ships to Endless Sky.

The intention is to build a **cohesive Babylon 5 universe** that takes advantage of Endless Sky's exploration, trading, combat, fleet, and mission systems.

That means creating a setting where:

* Factions feel politically and mechanically distinct.
* Different regions have meaningful economic differences.
* Ships and equipment reflect the strengths and weaknesses of their respective civilizations.
* Exploration provides useful world-building.
* The galaxy changes as the story progresses.
* The player can participate in a larger narrative while still having freedom to explore and trade.

The current Part 1 development effort is building the foundation required to support that larger goal.

## Roadmap

Development is iterative, and this roadmap is intentionally broad.

### Part 1 — Foundation and Opening Campaign

* Establish the major Babylon 5 factions.
* Build the core galaxy map.
* Implement faction ships and equipment.
* Establish colonies and regional economies.
* Create player starts.
* Build the opening campaign.
* Establish the characters and events leading toward the larger conflict.
* Improve isolation from vanilla Endless Sky content.
* Replace development artwork.

### Future Development

Planned story development is intended to move toward:

* Expansion of the major faction storylines.
* Increased political and military activity.
* The Shadow War.
* The consequences of the Shadow War.
* The Earth Civil War.

The exact scope and sequence may change as development progresses.

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter a problem, please open an issue and include as much information as possible.

Useful reports include:

* The Endless Sky version you are using.
* The Endless B5 version or commit.
* What you were doing when the problem occurred.
* The system, planet, ship, or mission involved.
* Any relevant Endless Sky error messages.
* Steps that reproduce the problem.
* errors.txt from your installation directory.

Reports of unexpected **vanilla Endless Sky content appearing in the Babylon 5 setting** are particularly useful.  Things like random star port missions, pirate fleet engagements, etc etc.
